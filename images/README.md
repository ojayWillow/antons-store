# Product photos

Each fragrance has its own photo box on the site. Put one photo per scent here and it appears automatically after the next deploy:

| File | Scent |
|---|---|
| `01.jpg` … `19.jpg` | No. 01 … No. 19 |

- Format: JPG, portrait **4:5**, ideally **1200 × 1500 px**, under 300 KB.
- Plain or seamless background, bottle centred, same light for all 19 so the grid looks consistent.
- Until a photo exists, the site shows the drawn placeholder bottle with a dashed "photo slot" frame.
